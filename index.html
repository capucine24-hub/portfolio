More about me

The slightly longer version

I’m drawn to digital experiences that don’t just work, they feel good. I love that moment when an interface suddenly makes sense, when design and technology come together naturally.

I’m especially interested in the future of sport experiences: how technology could change the way fans watch, feel, share and connect around live events. Rugby, F1 and sport culture are big sources of inspiration for me.

I like building things that feel useful, playful and human. The kind of work I want to make is not only functional it should make people curious, bring them together, or simply make them smile.

I’m still learning, still experimenting, and still figuring out exactly where I fit. But I know I want to build experiences that people remember because of how they made them feel.
