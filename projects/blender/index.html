01

Project overview

A Seasonal Encounter: The Final Shift is a short Blender animation exploring the emotional experience of entering university.

The story follows Atlas as he meets Nyx and Thallo across a shifting seasonal landscape, using environment, movement and atmosphere to represent excitement, anxiety, acceptance and growth.

Summer

Excitement, arrival and possibility.

Winter

Challenges, anxiety and emotional pressure.

Autumn

Acceptance, reflection and transition.

Spring

Growth, calm and personal change.

02

Characters & symbolism

Atlas represents endurance and the student experience. He is the emotional anchor of the animation, moving through a new environment while trying to understand his place within it.

Nyx symbolises darkness, pressure and the more overwhelming side of transition. Thallo represents growth, calm and understanding.

Together, the characters helped turn an internal emotional shift into something visual, physical and atmospheric.

03

Building the scene

The project combined 3D modelling, character animation, lighting, texturing, camera movement and environmental effects.

I modelled the two alien characters, Nyx and Thallo, from scratch, created the amphitheatre environment, animated the scene using keyframing and armatures, and experimented with inverse kinematics for character movement.

I also worked with falling leaves, snow, wind forces, turbulence, UV textures, bump mapping and cloth simulation, including Nyx’s cape.

Tools & techniques

Blender 3D modelling Character design Armatures Keyframe animation UV mapping Bump mapping Cloth simulation Particle effects Lighting Camera animation

04

Production decisions

The final animation changed from the original storyboard because of time, rendering and technical constraints. Some planned elements, such as the clock, laser-beam words and handshake, were simplified or removed.

The final output remained closer to a prototype render than a fully polished animation, so I prioritised atmosphere, character movement, seasonal transitions and the emotional rhythm of the scene.

05

Reflection

This project helped me understand how 3D animation can communicate emotion through lighting, movement, weather, camera framing and character design.

It also made me more comfortable with Blender’s production workflow, from modelling and rigging to scene building, animation and rendering.
